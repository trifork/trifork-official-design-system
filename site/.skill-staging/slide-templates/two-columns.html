APPROACH

How we worked.

Two principles that shaped every architecture review and every sprint plan.

Distributed by design

Every service was multi-region from day one. Cross-region failover ran continuously in pre-production; we never built it as a feature.

The trade-off, eventual consistency on read paths, was negotiated with the operations desk before the first line of code.

Built to be operated

Observability, runbooks, and on-call tooling were part of the brief. Every feature merged with its alerts, dashboards, and SLO already wired in.

By handover, the bank's own SREs had been on rotation for ten weeks alongside our team.
